Certified electricians who know North West London.

North West London is our home patch: Phoenix is based on Brondesbury Road in NW6, so Kilburn, West Hampstead, Queen's Park and Cricklewood are minutes away and Hampstead, Hendon and Mill Hill are a short drive. The area's Edwardian houses and mansion flats often still carry original wiring behind newer sockets, so a lot of our work here starts with an EICR for a sale or a let and ends with a consumer unit upgrade, a partial rewire, or an EV charger once the board can take it.

  • Sales & purchases: EICRs and fast remedials that unblock a sale, like the Brondesbury terrace we certified in three days before exchange.
  • Hampstead & St John's Wood: careful rewires in high-spec homes, with chasing and making good planned around decorators.
  • Hendon, Mill Hill, NW9: driveway EV chargers, often alongside a new consumer unit ready for a future battery or heat pump.
  • Landlords: five-yearly EICRs across NW6, NW2 and NW10 lets, with reminders before each one expires.
