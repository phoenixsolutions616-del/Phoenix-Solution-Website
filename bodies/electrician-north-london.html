Certified electricians who know North London.

North London's Victorian and Edwardian terraces, mansion blocks and converted flats keep us busy with a particular mix of work: tired fuse boards in houses that have been extended several times, partial rewires during loft and kitchen projects, and EICRs for the many rented flats between Islington and Wood Green. Further out, in Finchley, Southgate and Winchmore Hill, more homes have driveways, and that is where most of our North London EV charger installations happen.

  • Period terraces: consumer unit upgrades and partial rewires timed around refurbishments, with circuits labelled properly at last.
  • Rented flats: landlord EICRs and same-month remedials, so certificates go to tenants within the 28 days the law allows.
  • Driveway homes: home EV chargers with load management where the supply is shared with an electric shower or heat pump.
  • Blocks & agents: portfolio EICRs and communal fire alarm and emergency lighting on one schedule, as in our 38-flat Islington takeover.
