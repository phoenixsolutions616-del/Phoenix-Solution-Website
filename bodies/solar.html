Use your sunshine after sunset.

Without storage, most homes export half their solar generation for pennies and buy it back at full price in the evening. A battery closes that loop, capturing your daytime surplus and releasing it when you actually need it, then topping up from cheap overnight tariffs in winter.

As certified Tesla Powerwall installers we fit Powerwall 3 with its integrated inverter and whole-home backup capability, alongside modular systems from GivEnergy and Fox ESS that let you start at 5kWh and grow. We'll size your battery from your smart meter data, not guesswork, oversized batteries waste money, undersized ones waste sunshine.

  • Whole-home backup: with Powerwall's Backup Gateway, a power cut becomes a non-event.
  • Tariff arbitrage: charge at 7p overnight, use it at peak instead of paying 28p; batteries pay back even without solar.
  • Retrofit friendly: AC-coupled batteries add storage to existing solar arrays without touching your panels.
  • Smart integration: batteries, EV charger and immersion diverter orchestrated so nothing fights for the same surplus.

Straight answers on solar and storage

South is ideal but far from essential. East and west roofs each produce around 80-85% of a south-facing equivalent, and an east-west split actually spreads generation across more of the day, often matching a household's usage better than a single midday peak. North-facing-only roofs are the one orientation we'll usually talk you out of.

Most residential solar falls under permitted development, so no application is needed. Exceptions include listed buildings, some conservation areas and flats. We check your property's status during the desktop assessment, and where permission is needed, or where a flush in-roof system would satisfy planners, we'll guide you through it.

If you're out during the day, a battery typically lifts your self-consumption from around 35% to 70%+ and is usually worth including from the start, especially at 0% VAT when installed with solar. If your daytime usage is high (home workers, heat pumps), panels alone perform well and a battery can be AC-coupled later. We'll model both options so you're choosing with numbers, not vibes.

Standard grid-tied solar shuts down in a power cut for safety. If backup matters to you, we can install a battery system with backup capability, from a single essential circuit through to Tesla Powerwall's whole-home Backup Gateway, which switches over so fast most appliances never notice.

Very little: London rain does most of the cleaning, panels have no moving parts, and inverters typically run 10-15 years before replacement. We recommend an annual visual and electrical health check, included in our care plan, plus a panel clean every couple of years if your roof pitch is shallow or you're near trees.

An owned (not leased) solar system with proper MCS paperwork is consistently viewed as an asset: lower running costs improve your EPC rating, and buyers increasingly ask for it.
