Signs your home needs rewiring.

Wiring doesn't come with an expiry date, but insulation ages, standards change, and houses get extended and altered until the original circuits can't cope. These are the signs we look for, and the ones an EICR will usually pick up.

  • Old cable types: rubber, fabric or lead-sheathed cables, which crumble with age.
  • Red and black wiring: cable colours used before 2004, a sign the circuits are at least twenty years old.
  • No earth on lighting: common in older houses and a problem when you fit metal light fittings.
  • Too few sockets: extension leads in every room, or one ring circuit for the whole house.
  • Warning signs: scorch marks, warm sockets or switches, a burning smell, or lights that flicker.
  • An unsatisfactory EICR: multiple C2 codes across several circuits often make a rewire better value than patching.

Full rewire or partial rewire?

Not every older home needs everything replaced. We test before we quote, so you only pay for the circuits that need it.

Full rewire

Every circuit replaced from the consumer unit outwards: sockets, lighting, cooker, shower, smoke alarms and outdoor power, plus a new consumer unit. Best when the cable itself is old, before a major renovation, or when an empty property gives clear access. A typical three-bedroom house takes one to two weeks, like the 1930s semi in Ealing we rewired in nine days with fourteen new circuits.

Partial rewire

Selected circuits replaced: a kitchen during a refit, a loft conversion, an extension, or lighting circuits with no earth. Often combined with a consumer unit upgrade so the whole installation is protected to the current standard, even where some of the original wiring stays.

How a Phoenix rewire works.

1. Survey & design

We test the existing installation and walk the house with you to plan sockets, lighting, data and any EV or heat pump supplies.

2. First fix

New cables run and chased in, back boxes fitted. Floors lifted and replaced carefully; rooms finished in a sequence agreed with you.

3. Second fix

Sockets, switches, lights and the consumer unit connected after plastering, ready for decorating.

4. Test & certify

Full testing to BS 7671, an Electrical Installation Certificate and Building Control notification under Part P.
