Certified electricians who know Enfield & Barnet.

The EN postcodes run from Enfield and Barnet out to Potters Bar, Cheshunt and Hoddesdon, and much of the housing is 1930s semis and post-war homes with driveways and garages. That makes it ideal territory for home EV charging. It also means many fuse boards here are original or 1980s replacements, so we often upgrade the consumer unit first and fit the charger the same day.

  • Semis with driveways: tethered or untethered EV chargers, cable runs through garages, and earthing done properly for the outdoor point.
  • 1930s houses: rewires where rubber or early PVC wiring is still in use, and consumer units with RCBOs and surge protection.
  • Potters Bar & Cheshunt: EICRs for buyers and landlords, with clear photos and plain-English notes on every observation.
  • Solar homes: solar-aware chargers like the Zappi that charge the car from surplus generation.
