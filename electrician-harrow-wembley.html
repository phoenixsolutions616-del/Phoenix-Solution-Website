Certified electricians who know Harrow & Wembley.

Harrow, Wembley and the Metro-land suburbs of Pinner, Ruislip and Stanmore are full of 1930s semis and detached homes with front drives, so EV charger installation is a big part of our work in the HA postcodes. Many of these houses also have large extensions added over the years on top of the original wiring, which shows up as overloaded boards, tripping RCDs and unsatisfactory EICRs.

  • Metro-land semis: home EV chargers fitted on the front elevation with neat, clipped cable runs and no trailing leads.
  • Extended homes: consumer unit upgrades that give extensions, kitchens and garden rooms their own protected circuits.
  • Wembley & Harrow lets: landlord EICRs and remedials completed inside the 28-day deadline.
  • Northwood & Stanmore: full and partial rewires in larger homes, planned room by room so you can stay living in.
